What is TrackYourTrades?

TrackYourTrades is a local-first trading journal. You log your closed trades, and the app gives you a clear picture of your performance — P&L, win rate, equity curve, streaks, and more.

There's no account to create, no server to trust, and no subscription. Your data lives in your browser.

How to Log Trades

Press + Log in the top right (or press N) to open the trade entry form. Fill in the date, ticker, entry price, exit price, and quantity. Hit Save.

You can also switch to the Option tab in the log form to record single-leg options trades — including call/put, strike, expiration, and contracts.

To import multiple trades at once, use the ↑ Import CSV button on the Trades page, or upload during onboarding. The importer handles TYT exports and broker statement CSVs.

Dashboard

The Dashboard shows your performance for the selected time range. Use the time buttons (1D, 1W, 1M, 3M, YTD, 1Y, ALL, Custom) to filter. The equity curve, daily P&L chart, streaks, and breakdown widgets all reflect the active range.

Use ⊞ Customize to drag, reorder, or hide widgets.

Use ⚑ Set Goal to define a dollar, percentage, or P&L target and overlay a goal line on the equity curve.

Calendar

The Calendar shows your trading days color-coded green (profitable) or red (losing). Click any day to see that session's trades and write a journal entry.

Click the month/year label to jump directly to any month. Use the arrow buttons to navigate one month at a time.

Switch between Month and Sessions view using the toggle in the top right of the calendar.

CSV Import

The importer is designed to handle both clean flat exports and messier broker statements (like Robinhood monthly PDFs exported to CSV).

What gets imported

Only actual executed buy/sell fills with a valid quantity and price. Completed trades only.

What gets skipped

Pending settlement rows, cash sweep activity, interest payments, Gold plan credits, deposit/withdrawal events, cancelled or partial fills, and any row missing a ticker, price, or quantity.

The importer always shows you exactly what was accepted, what was skipped as a duplicate, and what was skipped as non-trade data.

Terminology

P&L
Profit and loss. (Exit − Entry) × Qty − Fees.
Win Rate
Winning trades ÷ total trades.
Profit Factor
Gross wins ÷ gross losses. Above 1.0 is net positive.
Avg Winner
Average P&L of all winning trades.
Equity Curve
Your account value over time based on cumulative P&L.
Streak
Consecutive winning or losing trades.

Privacy & Storage

🔒 Local-first — no server, no tracking

All your data is stored in your browser's IndexedDB. Nothing is sent to any server. TrackYourTrades has no backend, no accounts, and no analytics on your trade data.

What this means for you

Personal device: Full privacy. Only you can see your data.

Shared or managed device: Your browser data may be visible to whoever manages that device (employer, family member, IT admin). If privacy matters, use a personal device.

Clearing browser data: If you clear your browser storage, site data, or use a private/incognito window, your trades will be lost. Export your data regularly as a backup.

Backup guidance

Use the ↓ CSV button to export your full trade history. Keep a copy in a safe location. The CSV is re-importable — it's your backup.
