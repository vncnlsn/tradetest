Quick Start

  1. Click + Log in the top right (or press N)
  2. Choose Manual or Upload CSV
  3. For manual: enter date, ticker, entry price, exit price, quantity. Hit Save.
  4. Your dashboard updates instantly — no reload needed

What This Is

  • A private trading journal and performance tracker
  • Tracks closed trades only — no open positions
  • Shows real performance based on what you actually did
  • No account required. No subscription. No server.

How to Log Trades

  • Use + Log or press N anywhere in the app
  • Choose Manual for single trades
  • Choose Upload CSV to import a batch at once
  • Toggle to Option mode for single-leg options: call/put, strike, expiration, contracts
  • Deposits and withdrawals are tracked separately and do not affect P&L

Dashboard

  • All metrics update based on the selected time filter
  • Return = filtered P&L divided by starting account value
  • Win Rate = winning trades divided by total trades in the period
  • Use Customize to drag, reorder, or hide widgets
  • Use Set Goal to overlay a target line on the equity curve

Calendar

  • Green day = profitable. Red day = loss.
  • Click any day to see that session's trades and write journal notes
  • Click the month label to jump directly to any month or year
  • Sessions view shows every day as a row — including journal previews
  • Day panel slides in on the right without leaving the calendar

CSV Import

What gets imported: completed buy and sell fills with valid price and quantity.

What gets skipped: cancelled orders, pending rows, cash sweeps, interest payments, and any row missing a ticker, price, or quantity.

Duplicates are detected automatically. Re-importing the same file is always safe.

Terminology

P&L
Profit and loss. (Exit minus Entry) times Quantity, minus fees.
Win Rate
Winning trades divided by total trades in the selected period.
Return
Filtered P&L divided by your starting account value.
Avg Winner
Average P&L across all winning trades in the period.
Equity Curve
Your account value over time based on cumulative closed P&L.
Streak
Consecutive winning or losing individual trades.

Why This Exists

  • Most trading tools are complicated, expensive, or subscription-only
  • This one is simple, fast, and built for real traders
  • Log a trade in under 10 seconds
  • See your real numbers without noise
  • Your data belongs to you, not a company

Privacy and Storage

All data is stored in your browser using IndexedDB. Nothing is sent to any server. TrackYourTrades has no backend, no user accounts, and no analytics on your trade data.
  • Personal device: Full privacy. Only you can access the data.
  • Shared or managed device: Your browser data may be visible to whoever manages that device. Use a personal device if privacy matters.
  • Clearing browser data: If you clear site data or use a private window, your trades will be gone. There is no cloud backup.
Export regularly. Use the CSV button in the top bar to download your full trade history. Keep a copy somewhere safe. The CSV is re-importable and is your only backup.
